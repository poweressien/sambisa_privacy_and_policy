This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow of Sambisa (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you play the Game. The Game is published by [YOUR NAME / STUDIO NAME] (“the Developer”). By installing or playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • The Game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device.
  • If you use the online co-op (squad) feature, your connection details (such as your IP address) are shared with the other player and with the connection services described below, only so the two devices can connect.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings (for example unlocked levels, coins, weapons, operators, skins, and audio or control settings) in local storage on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted if you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information on our own servers. The Game does not use advertising IDs, does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files or precise location.

4. Online co-op and network connections

The Game requests the Internet permission for its online co-op feature. When you host or join a squad using a squad code, the Game uses peer-to-peer (WebRTC) technology. To make this work:

  • A public matchmaking service (PeerJS Cloud) is used to exchange the squad code so the two devices can find each other.
  • Public STUN and relay (TURN) servers may be used to help devices on different networks connect. These services necessarily see your IP address and connection metadata. Operators of these services include PeerJS, Google, Twilio and Open Relay (Metered).
  • During a co-op session, gameplay data (such as player positions and actions) is exchanged directly between the devices in the squad. Your IP address may be visible to the other player in your squad.

We do not control these third-party services and do not receive this data. Please review their own privacy policies for how they handle connection data. You can avoid all of this by playing in single-player mode and not using co-op.

5. Sharing an invite

If you choose to share your squad code, the Game can open an app you pick, such as WhatsApp, with a pre-filled message. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send it, and what happens next is governed by that app’s privacy policy.

6. In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases (for example operators) through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so that the item can be unlocked on your device. Google’s handling of your payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet: for the online co-op feature and for purchases through Google Play.
  • Vibration: to provide haptic feedback during gameplay.

8. Children’s privacy

The Game contains stylised fictional violence and is not designed for or directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. To remove all data the Game keeps on your device, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the Game.

10. Security
